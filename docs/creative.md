# Act: Creative

## Search Suggestions

The header search loads matching questions after a short typing delay. Users can choose a suggestion with the mouse or keyboard, or open all results. Old requests are cancelled when the query changes.

## Favorites and Subscriptions

Signed-in users can save questions to Favorites and follow them by email. The side menu opens both lists. Subscription emails are sent by the backend.

## Reactions and Sharing

Questions and comments support likes and dislikes. A question card can share its link through the browser share dialog or copy it to the clipboard.

## Comment Updates

An open question listens for server events and reloads its comments when changes arrive. The connection is closed when leaving the page and can reconnect after a temporary failure.
